<p>The preferred way to get support from the MRBS development team is through the <a href="https://github.com/meeting-room-booking-system/mrbs-code/issues">GitHub issue tracker</a>.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://github.com/meeting-room-booking-system/mrbs-code/discussions">GitHub Discussions forum</a> is used for announcements and community discussion. It is a good place to follow news about MRBS, share ideas and talk with other users.</p>

<p>Please do not contact developers or administrators directly. The project is maintained in the team's spare time, and using the issue tracker gives your question the best chance of receiving a useful, timely response.</p>
