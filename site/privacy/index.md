<p>This website provides information about MRBS. It has no accounts, forms or comments.</p>

<h2>Analytics</h2>

<p>If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics (GA4), a service provided by Google, to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited and which countries visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies named <code>_ga</code> and <code>_ga_*</code> in your browser and sends usage data, including your IP address, to Google. We use this information only to improve the site.</p>

<p>Nothing is sent to Google, and no analytics cookies are set, unless you click <strong>Accept</strong> on the cookie banner. If you click <strong>Decline</strong>, or ignore the banner, no analytics are used.</p>

<h2>Your choice</h2>

<p>Your choice is stored in your browser (in local storage, under the name <code>mrbs-analytics-consent</code>) so that you are not asked on every visit. You can change it at any time with the <strong>Cookie settings</strong> link in the page footer. Declining after previously accepting removes the analytics cookies.</p>

<h2>Other services</h2>

<p>Fonts and other assets are served from this site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses as described in the <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub Privacy Statement</a>.</p>

<p>Links to other sites, such as GitHub, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.</p>
