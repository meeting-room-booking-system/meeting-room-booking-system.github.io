<section class="section-band">
  <div>
    <p class="eyebrow">Built for shared spaces</p>
    <h2>Booking without the paper trail.</h2>
    <p>MRBS brings room calendars, booking rules, and administration together in a browser-based system.</p>
  </div>
  <ul class="feature-list">
    <li>Available through a browser on web and intranet installations</li>
    <li>Flexible repeating bookings</li>
    <li>Authentication with existing user databases</li>
    <li>Conflict prevention and booking reports</li>
    <li>Day, week, and month views</li>
    <li>Bookings by time or period</li>
    <li>Read-only, user, and admin access levels</li>
    <li>Email notifications for room administrators</li>
    <li>Many community-supported languages</li>
    <li>MySQL and PostgreSQL support</li>
  </ul>
</section>

  </div>
